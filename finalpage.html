Buggu Day ❤️

Buggu...

I know Girlfriend's Day has passed.
And I know that day cannot come back.

But I was thinking...
why wait for one special day every year?

Why don't we create our own little day?

A small day that belongs only to us.
Our little tradition.

Our Buggu Day ❤️

☕ Our Coffee Date

You make your favorite coffee...
I make mine...
and we sit together while talking about our day.

Like two people sitting beside each other, even if there are miles between us.
🍝 Our Pasta Time

We both cook pasta together.
You cook yours...
I cook mine...
and then we show each other our creations.

Maybe we laugh at each other's cooking, but that is the fun part ❤️
🤍 Our Little World

No big plans.
No perfect moments.
Just you and me,
talking, laughing, sharing stories, and enjoying our time together.

Buggu, I am sorry...

Sorry that I couldn't make that day feel as special
as you deserved.

I know sometimes you don't need big things...
you just want to feel loved, remembered, and important.

And I want you to know...
your happiness, your smile, and those little moments when you are excited about things you love...
they matter to me.

So let's make our own memories, Buggu ❤️

A small coffee.
A little pasta.
A lot of talking.
And a little piece of us together.

I love you ❤️
