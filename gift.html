A Little Memory ❤️

Do you remember those two days...

when we were choosing what to get?

It was not just about buying something.
It was about sitting together...
looking at different options...
and imagining what would make you happy.

💄

I always notice that little happiness on your face...
when you get something related to makeup and beauty.
✨

Seeing you excited about these little things,
your vanity collection,
your little space of things you love...
makes me happy too.

Maybe one day when you are here...

we will build that little corner together.
A mirror,
your favorite products,
and your smile in front of it ❤️

That is the moment I am waiting for.
